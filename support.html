Support & FAQ

How do I log an outbreak?

Tap the + button (top-right of the Home tab, or "I feel a tingle" in quick actions) the moment you feel the prodrome — the tingling sensation that precedes a visible sore. Early logging is what makes trigger correlations meaningful.

How does trigger correlation work?

Each time you log an outbreak, you toggle which triggers happened in the previous 48 hours (sun, stress, illness, poor sleep, menstrual cycle, spicy food, dehydration, dental work, new lip product, intimate contact). After two or more outbreaks, the Triggers tab shows which triggers preceded the most outbreaks — your personal correlation map. This is observational, not diagnostic.

Where are my photos stored?

On your device only. LipBeacon does not upload your photos anywhere. They live in the App's sandbox in your iPhone's encrypted storage. They are deleted when you delete the App. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → LipBeacon → Cancel Subscription. You can also tap "Manage subscription" inside the App's Settings tab.

I cancelled but I'm still being charged.

All LipBeacon subscription billing is handled by Apple. If you see an unexpected charge, the official path is reportaproblem.apple.com — Apple handles all refunds. LipBeacon does not have access to your payment details.

I bought Pro but the app still asks me to upgrade.

Open Settings inside LipBeacon and tap "Restore purchases". This pulls your subscription state from your Apple ID. If it still doesn't unlock, please email us with your Apple ID's order confirmation.

Is LipBeacon a medical app?

No. LipBeacon is a personal observation tool. It does not diagnose herpes simplex virus, does not assess severity, and does not recommend treatment. Always consult a licensed clinician for any health concern.

Do you have an Android version?

Not yet. LipBeacon V1 is iOS only.

Need something else?

Email support@lipbeacon.com. We read everything and aim to reply within 48 hours.
