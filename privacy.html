Privacy Policy

Effective May 13, 2026

LipBeacon ("we", "us", "the App") is an iPhone app for personal outbreak tracking. We do not have a backend server. We do not have user accounts. This privacy policy explains the limited data the App collects and how it is handled.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter into LipBeacon — outbreak logs, photos, triggers, medication entries, notes — is stored locally in your iPhone's encrypted on-device storage (Apple SwiftData + the Documents directory). This data:

  • Is never transmitted to LipBeacon's servers (we don't operate any).
  • Is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Is not analyzed, profiled, or used for advertising.
  • Is deleted when you delete the app.

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your iPhone, your LipBeacon data may be included in your encrypted iCloud Backup per your Apple ID's settings. This is controlled entirely by Apple, not by us.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any account identifier.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • We do not embed third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude).
  • We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs.

Purchases & subscriptions

If you subscribe to LipBeacon Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. Apple is responsible for the transaction, billing, and the receipt verification. LipBeacon receives only the entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features locally on your device. Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy covers that transaction.

Camera & photo library access

If you choose to add a healing-progress photo, the App will ask for permission to use your camera or photo library. Photos you add are saved to LipBeacon's on-device sandbox only. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Face ID

If you enable the Face ID lock in Settings, biometric authentication is handled by Apple's Local Authentication framework. LipBeacon never sees your biometric data — we only receive a yes/no signal from iOS indicating whether authentication succeeded.

Children

LipBeacon is rated 17+ in the App Store and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Medical disclaimer

LipBeacon is for personal tracking only. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose any condition, and is not a substitute for advice from a licensed healthcare professional. Always consult a qualified clinician for any medical concern.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in any material way, we will update the effective date above and post the new version here. There is no in-app email collection, so we cannot notify you directly — please check back periodically.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@lipbeacon.com.
